# Collect a creator's game takes (for Claude in Chrome)

Used to backtest the notes checker on Weeks 1–3 with someone else's takes before you have
your own notes. Paste everything below the line into Claude in Chrome. Paste its reply back
into the Claude Code session, which turns the takes into claim-format notes files in
`private/notes/<tag>_<source>.md` (gitignored: someone else's takes stay private).

---

Collect **Bobby Skinner's** (@BobbySkinner_ on X, host of the Giants Nation Show) takes about
the **New York Giants' 2026 Weeks 1–3 games**:
- Week 1: vs DAL, Sun 9/13 (NYG won 28–20)
- Week 2: at LA Rams, Mon 9/21 (NYG won 28–6)
- Week 3: vs TEN, Sun 9/27 (NYG won 12–7)

## Rules
- **Read only.** Don't like, repost, reply, follow, bookmark or DM anything. Don't log in or out.
- **Human pace.** One tab. Scroll slowly, and pause at least 5 seconds between scrolls or
  page loads. Cap: about 30 minutes and 40 page loads/scroll batches. Stop at the cap.
- Use the profile page and X's normal search box only
  (e.g. `from:BobbySkinner_ since:2026-09-13 until:2026-09-30`). No dev tools or API tricks.
- If you see a rate-limit, "something went wrong, try again", a CAPTCHA or a login wall,
  **stop**, don't retry, and report what you have.
- **Copy text verbatim.** Don't paraphrase, merge or "improve" takes. Never invent a take.
  If you're unsure a post is his or is about these games, leave it out.

## What to collect
Posts from game day through the Thursday after each game, where he makes a **checkable
claim** about what happened: a player's performance, the O-line or defense, scheme or
play-calling, coaching decisions, penalties. Skip pure hype, jokes, injury news, schedule
posts and reposts of other people (quote-posts where he adds his own take are fine).
Also note any clearly labeled **pre-game predictions** separately.

## Reply format (exactly this)
For each game, a list:

```
### Week N vs OPP
- [post-game] 2026-09-14 | https://x.com/BobbySkinner_/status/... | "exact post text"
- [pre-game]  2026-09-12 | https://x.com/BobbySkinner_/status/... | "exact post text"
```

End with: total posts scanned (roughly), posts kept, and anything you couldn't reach.
